Overnight shift — floor plan

Drawn from the live constants in scripts/main.gd. Same coordinates the game renders.

Cold vault (destination, back wall) Coffee / hot food island (warm) Dry goods gondola (odd) Snacks gondola Front counter Restroom Back room + mop Walkable aisle
  • Cold vault on the back wall. Cold drinks are what people came for, so the walk to the back is what makes them pass everything else.
  • Register just inside the door, facing the glass. Sight of the lot for the parking-lot incident, and sight of the door for theft.
  • Coffee island on the entrance sightline. Food service is visible the moment you walk in, with its own prep space.
  • 44 px between gondola runs — the playable version of the 42–48 inch retail aisle recommendation.
  • The counter cannot see the back corner. That blind spot is what makes a shoplifting incident possible at all.
  • Restroom and back room at the back. Service space behind the floor, which is where the mop lives.

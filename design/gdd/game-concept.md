# Closing Time — Game Concept

## Elevator pitch

Run one strange overnight shift at a roadside shop, read the customers, and decide who needs ordinary service, who needs help, and who should be refused before dawn.

## Pillars

1. Every customer presents an observable clue before asking for help.
2. Each meaningful choice changes cash, stock, trust, or the final dawn outcome.
3. The shop is small, readable, and atmospheric rather than a simulation spreadsheet.

## Anti-pillars

- No open world
- No multiplayer
- No crafting or farming
- No procedural town economy
- No giant dialogue tree

## Core loop

Inspect the shop → serve a customer → notice a clue → choose a response → see a consequence → make a limited restock decision → repeat until dawn.

## MVP slice

One shop, one 20–30 minute shift, 8–12 encounters, 10–12 products, one mystery thread, and three endings.

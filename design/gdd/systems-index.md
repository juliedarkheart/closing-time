# Systems Index

## MUST HAVE

1. **Player movement and interaction** — move through the shop and interact with shelves/register.
2. **Shelf stock system** — three visible categories with limited counts.
3. **Held item state** — taking an item removes it from a shelf and shows what the player is carrying.
4. **Customer service loop** — call customer, read request, retrieve item, return, serve.
5. **Useful question system** — one question per customer with a mechanical result.
6. **Consequences** — cash, shelf stock, trust, clues, and customer progress change visibly.
7. **Dawn summary and ending** — summarize service quality, trust, clues, and outcome.

## NICE TO HAVE

8. Restock order screen between customers
9. Animated customer sprites
10. Save/load
11. Accessibility options
12. Steam achievements
13. Weather and ambient audio
14. Alternate customer outcomes

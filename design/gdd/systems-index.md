# Systems Index

## MUST HAVE

1. **Player movement and interaction** — move through the shop and interact with shelves, register, bathroom, mop corner, and incidents.
2. **Real store floor plan** — parking and glass frontage, entrance right of centre, front counter facing the lot, coffee and hot food island on the entrance sightline, gondola runs down the middle, cold vault on the back wall, restroom and back room at the back.
3. **Shift clipboard** — the current objectives with tick marks, always on screen, counting progress against the shift totals.
4. **Proximity prompts** — every interactable names the object and the action when the player is in range.
5. **Fixture signage and zone readability** — identification labels on fixtures, distinct value and hue per district.
6. **Text that cannot clip** — wrap to the panel, size the panel to the text.
7. **Shelf stock system** — three visible categories with limited counts.
8. **Held item state** — taking an item removes it from a shelf and shows what the player is carrying.
9. **Opening chores** — two seeded tasks that must be completed at the correct fixture before the first customer.
10. **Visible customers** — customers enter through the door, walk the aisles, browse a shelf face, wait at the counter, and walk back out after service.
11. **Customer service loop** — call customer, read request, retrieve item, return, serve.
12. **Useful question system** — one question per customer with a mechanical result.
13. **Consequences** — cash, shelf stock, trust, clues, and customer progress change visibly.
14. **Night-shift incident system** — authored seeded incidents interrupt routine work with visible evidence in the world, then require inspection and a safe first response.
15. **Incident tools** — required equipment such as the mop gates whether a cleanup can be finished.
16. **Aftermath evidence** — resolved incidents leave visible traces instead of disappearing into text.
17. **Procedural shift generator** — one seed assembles the customer cast, incident schedule, starting stock, store condition, opening chores, and mystery thread.
18. **Shift replay** — start a new seeded shift without restarting the game.
19. **Dawn summary and ending** — summarize service quality, incidents, trust, clues, and outcome.

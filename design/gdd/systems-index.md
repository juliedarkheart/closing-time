# Systems Index

## MUST HAVE

1. **Player movement and interaction** — move through the shop and interact with shelves, register, bathroom, mop corner, and incidents.
2. **Shelf stock system** — three visible categories with limited counts.
3. **Held item state** — taking an item removes it from a shelf and shows what the player is carrying.
4. **Opening chores** — two seeded tasks that must be completed at the correct shelf before the first customer.
5. **Visible customers** — customers enter, browse a shelf, wander a second browse point, wait at the register, and leave after service.
6. **Customer service loop** — call customer, read request, retrieve item, return, serve.
7. **Useful question system** — one question per customer with a mechanical result.
8. **Consequences** — cash, shelf stock, trust, clues, and customer progress change visibly.
9. **Night-shift incident system** — authored seeded incidents interrupt routine work with visible evidence in the world, then require inspection and a safe first response.
10. **Incident tools** — required equipment such as the mop gates whether a cleanup can be finished.
11. **Aftermath evidence** — resolved incidents leave visible traces instead of disappearing into text.
12. **Procedural shift generator** — one seed assembles the customer cast, incident schedule, starting stock, store condition, opening chores, and mystery thread.
13. **Shift replay** — start a new seeded shift without restarting the game.
14. **Dawn summary and ending** — summarize service quality, incidents, trust, clues, and outcome.
